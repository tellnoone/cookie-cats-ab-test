A. LEGITIMATE CUT: userid decile (treatment-independent)
==========================================================================

  If userids are issued in install order, deciles approximate install
  cohorts. Crucially, a user's id cannot be affected by which gate they got,
  so this cut does not condition on anything the treatment touched.

  Segments significant at raw alpha = 0.05 : 1 of 10
  Segments significant after Benjamini-Hochberg : 0 of 10

  All 10 diffs negative? False
  Direction split: 8 negative, 2 positive

  This is the useful reading. The primary effect is small, so individual
  deciles at ~4,500 per arm are badly underpowered -- each has roughly a
  third of the sample needed to see a 0.8pp effect. Most deciles will look
  'not significant' whatever the truth is, and one or two may look large by
  chance. The consistency of the SIGN across deciles is more informative
  than any single decile's p-value.

==========================================================================
B. THE WRONG CUT: segmenting by sum_gamerounds (post-treatment)
==========================================================================

  Section 2 excluded this from the decision. Here is why, with numbers.

  `sum_gamerounds` is measured AFTER the gate takes effect. The gate is
  literally a device that stops people playing, so the treatment changes
  this variable. Splitting on it compares groups that are not like-for-like:
  a given bucket means something different in each arm.

     rounds    n ctl    n trt      ctl      trt   diff pp    p raw
          0    1,937    2,057   0.83%    0.63%    -0.194   0.4704
        1-5   10,119   10,604   1.29%    1.44%     0.148   0.3588
       6-20   12,428   12,657   5.03%    4.75%    -0.281   0.3027
      21-40    6,928    6,571  13.89%   12.72%    -1.163   0.0468
      41-80    5,750    5,793  30.45%   27.21%    -3.247   0.0001
     81-160    3,955    4,207  53.58%   51.96%    -1.617   0.1437
       160+    3,583    3,600  80.88%   80.94%     0.063   0.9463

  What is actually wrong here needs stating carefully, because the obvious
  reading is not the right one.

  2 of 7 buckets show a positive difference, but they are
  the two smallest effects in the table:
        1-5 : +0.148 pp  (p = 0.3588)
       160+ : +0.063 pp  (p = 0.9463)
  Those are noise, not a sign reversal. This is NOT Simpson's paradox, and
  calling it that would be overclaiming -- the aggregate sign does not
  genuinely flip inside any well-populated bucket.

  The real distortion is one of MAGNITUDE:
    aggregate effect           : -0.82 pp
    worst bucket ( 41-80)      : -3.25 pp  (p = 0.0001)
    ratio                      : 4.0x the aggregate

  Conditioning on a post-treatment variable manufactures effects several
  times larger than the real one. A reader shown only that row would
  conclude the gate change costs mid-engagement players 3.2pp of retention,
  which is roughly four times the actual aggregate harm.

  The smoking gun -- bucket membership itself differs by arm:

       bucket    gate_30    gate_40    shift
            0     4.33%      4.52%   +0.19pp
          1-5    22.64%     23.31%   +0.67pp
         6-20    27.80%     27.82%   +0.02pp
        21-40    15.50%     14.45%   -1.05pp
        41-80    12.86%     12.73%   -0.13pp
       81-160     8.85%      9.25%   +0.40pp
         160+     8.02%      7.91%   -0.10pp

    chi-square on membership : 27.57, p = 0.0001

    Significant. The treatment MOVED people between buckets, which is
    direct proof that the conditioning variable is treatment-affected.

  That is the whole objection in one line. When the treatment changes which
  bucket you land in, comparing arms WITHIN a bucket is comparing different
  populations. The gate at level 30 stops control players earlier, so a
  control player who still reached 80+ rounds pushed through a gate while a
  treatment player who reached 80+ rounds did not. Same bucket, different
  kind of player.

  Had this cut not been excluded in advance, it would have supplied a
  tempting and false story with a very convincing p-value attached
  (p = 0.0001 on the worst bucket). Pre-registration is what
  makes it unavailable.

==========================================================================
SEGMENT CONCLUSION
==========================================================================

  No segment changes the recommendation, and none was permitted to. The
  userid cut shows a consistent sign with intervals too wide to say more.
  The rounds cut shows why the plan excluded it.

  If anything here were to be pursued, it would be pursued as a NEW
  pre-registered experiment with its own primary metric and its own MDE.
