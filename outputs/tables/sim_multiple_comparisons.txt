==========================================================================
1. THE ARITHMETIC
==========================================================================

  If all 5 null hypotheses are true and the tests are independent, the
  chance of at least one false positive is:

    1 - (1 - 0.05)^5  =  1 - 0.95^5  =  0.2262

  So 22.6%, not 5%. Testing five metrics at 0.05 each means
  roughly a 1-in-4 chance of finding a 'winner' that does not exist.

    k metrics   P(>=1 false positive)
            1                   5.0%
            2                   9.8%
            3                  14.3%
            5                  22.6%
           10                  40.1%
           20                  64.2%

==========================================================================
3. WHAT CORRECTION COSTS (two metrics genuinely move)
==========================================================================

  Correction is not free. Here metrics 1 and 2 have a true +1.0pp effect
  and metrics 3-5 are null. Power is the chance of detecting a REAL effect.

    metric   true effect   uncorrected   Bonferroni       BH
         1        1.0pp       71.94%       48.04%   54.74%   <- real
         2        1.0pp       71.38%       47.14%   53.56%   <- real
         3        0.0pp        5.38%        0.86%    2.18%   <- null
         4        0.0pp        4.78%        1.12%    2.34%   <- null
         5        0.0pp        4.98%        1.12%    2.42%   <- null

  Averaged over the metrics that genuinely moved (POWER, higher is better):
    uncorrected        : 71.66%
    Bonferroni         : 47.59%
    Benjamini-Hochberg : 54.15%

  Averaged over the null metrics (FALSE POSITIVES, lower is better):
    uncorrected        : 5.05%
    Bonferroni         : 1.03%
    Benjamini-Hochberg : 2.31%

  This is the trade, and it is why BH is usually preferred to Bonferroni
  for a family of reported metrics: it controls the false DISCOVERY rate
  rather than the family-wise error rate, giving up less power for a
  slightly weaker guarantee.

  But note what neither correction can do: recover the clarity of having
  said in advance which metric decides. That is the point of section 2
  naming a single primary metric -- correction is damage control, and
  pre-specification is prevention.
