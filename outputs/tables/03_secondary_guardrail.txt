  1-day retention shows no significant difference.

  Interpretation per section 8 item 3. The pattern that would have worried us
  is 1-day UP with 7-day flat -- novelty. That is not what happened: both
  horizons point the same way (down).

  Stated precisely, because the CI crosses zero: 1-day retention is NOT
  significantly different (p = 0.0744, CI includes 0). This is therefore
  weak corroboration of the primary result, not independent confirmation.
  The honest summary is 'consistent in sign, inconclusive on its own'.

  One thing worth noticing -- the harm GROWS with the horizon:
    1-day : -0.59 pp on a 44.8% base   = -1.3% relative
    7-day : -0.82 pp on a 19.0% base   = -4.3% relative

  A gate moved later costs little by day 1 and more by day 7. That fits the
  competing hypothesis in section 1 -- that the gate does something
  structural whose absence compounds -- better than a novelty story, which
  would predict the opposite shape.

Guardrail: outlier diagnosis
==========================================================================

  Control arm's three largest values : 49,854  2,961  2,438
  Treatment arm's largest value      : 2,640

  userid 6390605 (control) played 49,854 rounds in a 14-day window.
  That is 16.8x the next-highest player in the dataset and works
  out to ~3,561 rounds/day, about 148 per hour sustained
  around the clock for two weeks. This is not a human play pattern; it is a
  bot or a logging fault.

  Why it matters for the guardrail specifically:
    control SD    :      256.7   vs treatment      103.3
    control skew  :      163.7   vs treatment        6.0
  The control arm looks far more variable than the treatment arm almost
  entirely because of this one row.

  Difference in means, all data        : -1.1575 rounds
  Difference in means, outlier removed : -0.0433 rounds

  The entire mean gap is that one player. Removing a single row out of
  90,189 moves the difference in means by 1.11 rounds and takes it to
  approximately zero. Any guardrail conclusion resting on the mean would
  have been a conclusion about one bot.

  NOTE: outlier handling was not pre-specified. Logged as a deviation in
  ANALYSIS_PLAN.md section 9. Both figures are reported; neither is hidden.

==========================================================================
Guardrail: Mann-Whitney U on the distributions
==========================================================================

  U statistic : 1,009,027,050
  p-value     : 0.0502
  median      : 17.0 (ctl) vs 16.0 (trt)

  Common-language effect size : 0.4962
  A randomly chosen gate_40 player plays more rounds than a randomly chosen
  gate_30 player 49.6% of the time (50% = no difference).

  Mann-Whitney tests stochastic dominance, not the mean, so it is immune to
  the single bot. It is the more trustworthy of the two tests here.

  p = 0.0502 sits essentially ON the 0.05 line. Calling that 'not
  significant' is as arbitrary as calling p = 0.0498 significant, so this
  verdict does not rest on the p-value. With n = 90,189 a vanishingly small
  shift is enough to push p to the boundary; the effect size is the
  informative quantity, and at 0.4962 it is negligible.

  GUARDRAIL VERDICT: no MATERIAL degradation in engagement.

  What that rests on, in order:
    - Common-language effect size 0.4962, against 0.5 for no difference.
      A 0.4% deviation from a coin flip is not a collapse in engagement.
    - Medians differ by 1 round (17 vs 16).
    - The mean gap is one bot, and vanishes when it is removed.
    - Both bootstrap CIs on the difference in means straddle zero.

  There is likely a real but tiny downward shift in rounds played: the sign
  agrees with both retention metrics, and that consistency is itself weak
  evidence. 'Material' is the word the plan chose, and a shift this small
  does not meet it.

  So the guardrail does not independently block a ship decision, and it does
  not rescue the primary result either. Worth naming what the guardrail was
  for: catching retention bought AT THE COST of engagement. Here retention
  FELL and engagement was flat -- not the trade-off it was designed to
  detect, and not a case where it has anything to veto.
